I’m a software developer at the [DOE Joint Genome Institute](https://jgi.doe.gov/) at [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://www.lbl.gov/). I’m part of the Data Science and Informatics department, where I currently work on [JAWS](https://jaws-docs.jgi.doe.gov/en/latest/). Previously, I worked at the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/) in the [Proteomics Platform](https://www.broadinstitute.org/proteomics).

Beyond work, I enjoy spending time in nature and learning about natural phenomena, particularly weather and natural hazards. I am also interested in finding creative ways to understand and translate systems, and dissecting how people tell stories, technical or otherwise, across different media and formats. I care deeply about animal rights and welfare, and share my home with three cats: Billy, Gwen, and Jet.
