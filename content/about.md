I’m a full-stack software developer at the [DOE Joint Genome Institute](https://jgi.doe.gov/) of [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://www.lbl.gov/), where I’ve worked since 2020. I currently work on [JAWS](https://jaws-docs.jgi.doe.gov/en/latest/), a workflow orchestration platform for running large-scale scientific analyses across high-performance computing resources.

Previously, I worked in the [Proteomics Platform](https://www.broadinstitute.org/proteomics) at the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/) from 2018 to 2020, after completing an M.S. in Computer Science at New York University’s [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://cims.nyu.edu/).

Beyond work, I enjoy spending time in nature and learning about natural phenomena, particularly weather and natural hazards. I am also interested in finding creative ways to understand and translate systems, and dissecting how people tell stories, technical or otherwise, across different media and formats. I care deeply about animal rights and welfare, and share my home with three cats: Billy, Gwen, and Jet.
